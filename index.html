Stefano Fiscale

Stefano is a Machine Learning Engineer at Kineton, where he develops Deep Learning models for applications in the Automotive field.

He has a PhD from the International PhD Programme UNESCO Chair on Environment, Resource and Sustainable Development at Parthenope University of Naples, working in the Department of Science and Technology. His doctoral thesis focused on the development of Machine Learning algorithms for exoplanet detection in Kepler and TESS data. He also served as principal observer in different projects aimed at supporting the ESA's Comet Interceptor mission, performing observations at ground-based telescopes: Asiago Copernico and Telescopio Nazionale Galileo.

He obtained his master's degree in Machine Learning and Big Data at Parthenope University of Naples, Italy (July 2022).

As he is very keen on improving his communication skills (e.g. giving talks, effective writing), he attended several courses and schools mainly held by Nature Masterclasses.
