Stefano Fiscale

Stefano is a Machine Learning Engineer in Kineton’s R&D department, where he is currently developing Reinforcement Learning models for applications in the Automotive field

He completed his PhD (’25) with the Doctor Europaeus label from Parthenope University of Naples, focusing his research on the development of Deep Learning models for exoplanet detection. He obtained his MSc degree in Machine Learning and Big Data (’22) and a BSc degree in Computer Science (’19) both from Parthenope University of Naples.

During his PhD he spent a three-month research stay at the Laboratoire d’Astrophysique de Marseille. He also served as principal observer in projects supporting ESA’s Comet Interceptor mission, performing observations at the Telescopio Nazionale Galileo and Asiago Copernico ground-based telescopes.

As he is very keen on improving his communication skills, he attended several courses and schools mainly held by Nature Masterclasses.

His discipline in scientific research is reflected in his daily life, placing great importance on physical and mental health, with balanced nutrition, early morning runs, strength training and meditation.
