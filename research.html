Research

Stefano Fiscale has authored and co-authored several research papers in machine learning, exoplanet detection, and cometary science. During his doctoral studies, he concentrated on the minimization of model complexity for exoplanet detection, developing simple, robust, and interpretable deep neural networks. Additionally, he was selected as lead observer for a research project supporting ESA’s Comet Interceptor mission, playing an integral role in the observation and characterization of target comets.
